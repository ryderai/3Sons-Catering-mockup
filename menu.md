## Specialties

### Slab Wrap $17

Brisket on warm naan bread topped with onions, tomato, cukes and Slabziki sauce.

### Texas Twinkies $14 GF

Three bacon-wrapped jalapeños stuffed with cheddar and cream cheeses, brisket and spices.

Takes an extra 5–7 minutes.

### Pitmaster’s Plate for Four $120

6 ribs, 1/3 lb. each of brisket, pulled pork, sausage, turkey and pulled chicken. Pints of mac & cheese, cole slaw, tater salad and beans, and two pieces of cornbread. No substitutions.

### No Sleep ’Til Brisket Burger $24

We only make a few of these each day, first come, first served. An 8 oz. brisket patty topped with 1/4 lb. of brisket and gruyère cheese on a brioche bun with lettuce, tomato, pickled onions, pickles and Peppah Rodgers sauce. Served with one side.

### Trailer Tacos $17 GF

Perfected in the original trailer. Two soft corn tortillas loaded with pulled pork, pulled chicken or brisket, fresh salsa, cole slaw and Sweet Georgia Brown sauce.

### Zapp’s Voodoo Bowl $16 GF

Zapp’s chips topped with pulled pork, cole slaw and Sweet Georgia Brown sauce.

### Brunswick Stew $9 / $16 / $30 GF

Cup, pint or quart. A closely guarded family recipe.

### Homemade Cornbread $5

Just like Grandma used to make.
