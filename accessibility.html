Accessibility

Everyone’s welcome at Mile Marker 1.

We want everyone to be able to visit 3 Sons Bar-B-Q, at the restaurant and on this website, including people with disabilities.

At the restaurant

  • A ramp up to the front deck
  • Wheelchair-accessible entrance
  • Wheelchair-accessible parking in our lot
  • Wheelchair-accessible restroom
  • Wheelchair-accessible seating

If you need anything else to make your visit easier, call us before you come and we’ll do our best to help.

On this website

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These are the guidelines most widely used to make websites work for people with disabilities, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or zoom. We review the site against them when we make design changes.

Need help?

If any part of this website is hard to use, or you need information in a different format, call (850) 278-6082 or email brisket@3sonsbarbq.com. We’ll help you place your order or get you what you need another way. Please tell us the page and the problem so we can fix it.

Other companies’ websites

Some things you can do from this site happen on other companies’ websites: online catering orders, gift card purchases, our menu service, delivery apps and job applications. We don’t control how those sites are built. If you have trouble with one, call us and we’ll help.
