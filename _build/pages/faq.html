3 Sons Bar-B-Q curates “Gulf Coast ’Q”, pulling elements from Georgia, the Carolinas, Memphis to Texas; we take pride in tradition. We smoke all of our meats on off-set smokers from Primitive Pits; low and slow is the key! Our menu includes classic choices like spare ribs, pulled pork, brisket, sausage, turkey and chicken. Additionally, we offer a variety of sides and desserts to complement your barbecue feast.
