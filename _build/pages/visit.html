01 Getting here

One mile off 98. Skip the 30A traffic.

Stay on US-98 and turn onto the west end of County Highway 30A. We’re one mile in, at Mile Marker 1 — look for the mile marker sign out front. You never have to drive through Seaside to get here.

03 When you get here

Bringing 10 people? Come on.

No reservations and plenty of room. Here’s what to expect, whether it’s lunch for two or the whole office.

Free

Parking

Park free in our own lot right out front. There’s free street parking nearby too, so there’s usually plenty of room.

99

Seats

Room for 99. Big groups are welcome. Just walk in.

Access

Wheelchair accessible

A ramp up to the front deck, plus a wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking, restroom and seating. Need anything else? Call before you come.

2 ways

How to order

Walk through our fast-moving Q line and sit wherever you like. Or belly up to the full-service bar and order there.

Kids

Bring the family

We have a kids’ menu for 12 and under, and high chairs if you need one.

To go

Taking it home?

We specialize in takeout. Come in and we’ll pack it up, or find us on 30A Grub to Go and DoorDash.

04 What to look for

The sign. The deck. The smoke.

A white building with a red-and-white sign out front, a big wooden deck with red chairs and umbrellas, and our own lot. When the lights come on at dusk, you can’t miss it.
