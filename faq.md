## Do you cater?

Yes, we do! We offer catering for various events and occasions. Whether you’re hosting a corporate event, family gathering, or a special celebration, our delicious barbecue dishes will surely impress your guests.

For medium orders (10–20 people), please give us a call; preferably 24 hours in advance so we can accommodate efficiently. For larger catering orders (30 or more people), we’ll need more time; please give us a call 48 hours in advance! We will have your order packed and ready for you to pick up and feed your crowd.

## What style of barbecue do you offer?

3 Sons Bar-B-Q curates “Gulf Coast ’Q”, pulling elements from Georgia, the Carolinas, Memphis to Texas; we take pride in tradition. We smoke all of our meats on off-set smokers from Primitive Pits; low and slow is the key! Our menu includes classic choices like spare ribs, pulled pork, brisket, sausage, turkey and chicken. Additionally, we offer a variety of sides and desserts to complement your barbecue feast.

## Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Riiight, we’ve been horrifying vegetarians since 2013!

But seriously, we do have our baked potato, salad and cole slaw. So your “friend” can still dine while you devour a succulent bar-b-q meal!

We offer many gluten-free options, and understand the importance of catering to different dietary preferences. Please let us know of any allergies ahead of ordering.

## Can I make a reservation for a large group?

We don’t offer reservations! When you arrive you can dine in one of two ways: Our bar is full service so feel free to belly up and enjoy. Alternatively, you’ll walk through our fast moving Q line and then sit down wherever you like. We have plenty of room for small and large groups alike!

## Do you offer takeout?

Yes, we specialize in takeout! Our service line is quick and efficient, so just come on in and we will pack up your order hot and fresh. For larger orders, please call us at (850) 278-6082 or stop in and we will have you on your way in no time.

## Do you offer delivery?

Yes, you can find us on 30A Grub to Go and Door Dash!

## What are your holiday hours?

We are closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Our operating hours on public holidays may vary. We recommend checking our website or calling us directly to confirm our hours of operation for specific holidays.

## Do you have a kids’ menu?

Yes, we have a kids’ menu designed to cater to our younger barbecue enthusiasts. Our kids’ menu features smaller portions of our popular dishes, as well as some kid-friendly favorites.

## More answers: parking, accessibility & happy hour

### Is there parking?

Yes. We have our own free parking lot on site, plus free street parking nearby, so there’s usually plenty of room.

### Is 3 Sons wheelchair accessible?

Yes. There’s a ramp up to the front deck, and the entrance, parking lot, restroom and seating are wheelchair accessible. If you need anything else, call us before you come.

### How many people can you seat?

We have 99 seats, so groups big and small are welcome. No reservations needed.

### When is happy hour?

Après runs 2p–5p every day at our full-service bar. We’re open 11a–8p daily. See happy hour
