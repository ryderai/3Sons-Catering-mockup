    06 Need to know

    Questions, answered.

    Still stuck? Call us.

    Call (850) 278-6082
    How much BBQ do I need for 20 people?

    Our 20-person package is 10 lbs. of meat (up to 3 choices) and 2.5 qts. each of 3 sides, with sauces and disposable serving utensils. It’s $440 + tax. An 18% gratuity is added to all catering orders.

    How far ahead should I order catering?

    Because we smoke the traditional way, we prefer 48 hours’ notice for bulk orders. More than 30 people: at least 72 hours. More than 50 people: call or email at least 5 days ahead. In a time crunch, call us — we may be able to work with your timeline.

    Do you take reservations?

    We don’t. Belly up to the full-service bar, or walk through our fast-moving Q line and sit wherever you like. There’s plenty of room for small and large groups.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes — find us on 30A Grub to Go and DoorDash.

    Do you have vegetarian or gluten-free options?

    We’ve been horrifying vegetarians since 2013. But we do have baked potatoes, salad and cole slaw, and many gluten-free options. Please tell us about allergies before you order.

    Are you open on holidays?

    We’re closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Other holiday hours can vary, so call ahead.
