01 Off the pit

Brisket is king. It’s not the only thing.

Our meat is smoked daily on our two 1,000-gallon Primitive Pits offset smokers, Buddy and Big Al, over hardwood oak. Then it goes on a plate, a wrap, a potato or a pile of Zapp’s chips.
